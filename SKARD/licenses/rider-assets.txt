SKARD • Arad character and board

The anatomical topology, child morph and starting deformation weights are adapted
from MakeHuman Community's hm08 base mesh, child target, and default skeleton.

The fitted facial triangle layout includes topology adapted from Google's
MediaPipe canonical face model under Apache License 2.0. The model was modified
for SKARD with local facial-landmark measurements, fitted volumetric geometry,
closed eyelid and lip loops, a skull-to-face seam, and a custom texture.
Copyright The MediaPipe Authors.
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj
Apache License text: mediapipe-Apache-2.0.txt

The fitted face uses measurements from the family's privately supplied portraits.
Its texture uses a cropped portrait, and selected details in the holiday fleece
and controller tee are sampled or redrawn from the family’s outfit reference.
The complete source photographs remain outside the game bundle.
The clothing shapes, original raccoon artwork, sticker treatment, helmet, board,
shoes, protective equipment, character posing, and skating animation were
authored or customized specifically for this build.
