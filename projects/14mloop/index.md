MeadowLoop is a 2014 MIT urban design studio proposal for New Jersey’s Meadowlands. The regional map locates dispersed warehouse sites. The packing diagram then tests a different arrangement: move those operations into a shared logistics complex near the junctions serving the port, airport and New York City.

Concentrating loading, storage and transfer space would free former warehouse sites for wetland restoration. The studio called those reclaimed landscapes “rooms” and the strips available for development “bands.” Its proposed commission boundary would put the rooms under a common system of management and maintenance.
