Virtual CityScope Champs-Élysées is an interactive and immersive platform for exploring the future of Paris' most important street. CityScope Champs-Élysées is a tangible user interface for urban planning and decision-making. The project explores new plans and future interventions for the Champs-Élysées Avenue, including reduced traffic, improved walkability, cycling and pedestrian mobility, and improved access to cultural landmarks and amenities. The physical exhibition opened at the Pavillon de l’Arsenal on February 14, 2020. When the venue closed during the pandemic, the project moved online. The venue later extended the exhibition to September 6. [Exhibition archive](https://www.pavillon-arsenal.com/en/expositions/11463-champs-elysees.html).
