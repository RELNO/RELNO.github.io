Ariel Noyman gave *Old, New Cities* at the European Commission Joint Research Centre’s City Attractiveness workshop in Brussels on December 1, 2017. The recording was published in 2019. The workshop asked how researchers could assess what draws people to a city and how that evidence could inform urban policy.

At [3:20 in the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AW-ljDEleLw&t=200s), a historical map of Paris introduces an example of how cities change over time. At [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AW-ljDEleLw&t=1050s), the CityScope Andorra example shows how a three-dimensional model can bring urban data into a planning discussion.
